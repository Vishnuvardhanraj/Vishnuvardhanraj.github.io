My analysis process

  1. 01

    Ask

    Pin down the real question and the decision it feeds.

  2. 02

    Collect

    Find the right sources and check they can answer it.

  3. 03

    Clean

    Fix gaps, duplicates and surprises. Write down every assumption.

  4. 04

    Explore

    Look for patterns, outliers and the story hiding in the rows.

  5. 05

    Model & visualize

    Test the idea, then show it in the simplest honest chart.

  6. 06

    Share & iterate

    Hand over a clear recommendation, then refine with feedback.
